About this demo

This is a proof of concept of the Burak oncology pre-cycle review, built on the existing Burak biomarker pipeline.

  • Illustrative data only. Every patient, value and date is fictional demonstration data. Nothing here describes a real person or a real clinical result.
  • Illustrative thresholds. Pre-cycle thresholds are common-practice examples. They are replaced by the hospital's own protocol, confirmed by its clinicians, before any use.
  • Decision support. Burak flags and explains. Every treatment decision stays with the treating oncologist.
  • Clear scope. Not for cancer screening, diagnosis or prediction, and it does not detect febrile neutropenia.
